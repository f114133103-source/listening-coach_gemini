📁 01. Project Introduction 遊戲背景與主要任務

Save the University Club Event! 拯救失蹤的社團活動企劃

The university club's annual event is right around the corner, but the master event plan has gone missing—and last-minute weather and venue emergencies have thrown the entire schedule into chaos! As a Campus Detective (校園小偵探), your main mission is to listen closely to authentic student conversations and voice messages across three progressive stages to recover key details, rebuild the schedule, and uncover the final rescue plan.

大學社團年度活動即將登場，但原始企劃書卻不翼而飛，加上突發的天氣與場地危機導致行程大亂！你將化身「校園小偵探」，透過聆聽三道關卡的英文對話與語音訊息，逐步蒐集線索、重組行程表，並破解最終活動密碼！

🎓 05. Learning Goals 核心聽力訓練目標 Find → Organize → Analyze
1. Listening for Specific Details
聆聽特定細節能力

Train ears to catch essential facts—dates, times, venues, and required items—within natural everyday English dialogues.

2. Information Organization
資訊整理與比對能力

Sort multi-turn schedules accurately while noticing speaker self-corrections and resisting distractor traps.

3. Inference & Problem-Solving
綜合推論與問題解決能力

Synthesize clues across multiple speakers to distinguish original plans from final decisions and solve real-world problems.
